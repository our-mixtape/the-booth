## 5. Positioning that survives all three perspectives

**Proposed headline:**

> A DJ booth you can perform in, with an AI partner that helps you prepare, adapt, and improve the next transition.

Show performance first. Then show the assistance changing a meaningful next action. If coaching leads, preserve learner ownership: a successful automated transition is not evidence of an unassisted skill gain.

The most defensible prospective contribution is the coupling of musical state, human control, adaptive planning, and repeatable practice. It is not the claim that anyone has invented 3D decks, stems, beatmatching, or an AI teacher for the first time.

### Competitive boundary

| Source-backed example | What it already advertises/documents | Implication for our claim |
|---|---|---|
| Tribe | Virtual DJ experiences, lessons, and an AI DJ coach in its browser offering.[^C1] | "3D decks plus an AI teacher" is insufficient novelty. |
| Algoriddim djay for Vision Pro | Spatial DJ equipment and hand-based interaction.[^C2] | "DJ with your hands" is insufficient novelty. |
| Sori public repository | Interactive musical instruments and Astra-assisted ensemble work, including numerical musical input and revisions.[^C3] | Numerical music representations and multi-instrument generation are not unique by themselves. |

These are scoped documentation comparisons, not hands-on competitive evaluations or claims that competitors lack every proposed feature. Sori's current repository does not establish exact submission-time functionality. Do not repeat earlier gallery counts or winner analyses without checking their original evidence.

Our proposed differentiating test: the same playing session receives a new constraint; the agent changes future assistance appropriately; manual control remains authoritative; a replay makes the result inspectable. We still need to demonstrate it.

## 7. Proposed internal scoring method

This is a rehearsal tool, not the organizers' undisclosed scoring scale.

Use a 0-5 evidence score for each official category: 0 absent; 1 assertion/mock; 2 partial loop; 3 working main path; 4 repeatable with evidence and clear limits; 5 strong evidence across a meaningful variation or failure case. With equal weights:

`internal_score_out_of_100 = 5 * (development + project + demo + technicality)`

Leave the total uncomputed when any category is unevaluated. A high total does not override an eligibility failure. Public access, allowed assets, contribution provenance, submission timing, and the required video are independent gates.

For each feature proposal, ask: which category benefits, what observable result will prove it, how long does it take, what could it break, and what simpler implementation would produce the same evidence? This favors a useful adaptive transition over a third visual environment.

Do not create a fabricated "Avery score," "Sriram score," or "Adina score." Use the questions above to pressure-test the same evidence packet.

## 8. Demo and Q&A preparation

### One-minute submission: proposed allocation

| Time | Evidence |
|---|---|
| 0-10 seconds | One sentence of user value; immediate audible control |
| 10-25 seconds | Stem-aware transition request grounded in the loaded tracks |
| 25-40 seconds | Change the request; show revised assistance and retained manual control |
| 40-50 seconds | One concrete outcome or focused retry; optional quick booth switch |
| 50-60 seconds | Identify event-built work and a specific Astra development contribution |

These are planning intervals, not measured response times. Record actual latency and disclose edits, prepared audio, annotations, assistance, and retries. Do not imply that a cut removes no meaningful waiting when it does.

### Three-minute finalist demo

Start with sound. Show the user action and model decision separately. Allow one bounded judge choice that changes the actual result. Demonstrate one recovery or retry, not every mode. Use the final moments for the technical/development evidence and immediate next validation step. Do not spend most of the time explaining investor biographies.

### Questions every teammate should answer

**Why Astra?** Name the actual feature used, the decision it improves, and observed evidence. Do not claim that a prompt-only fallback demonstrated native steering.

**What runs locally?** Playback, timing, controls, and enforcement. **What is AI?** The measured planning/coaching behavior and demonstrated development contributions. **What is preprocessing?** Separation, analysis, and any prepared annotations, disclosed by origin.

**Why not existing DJ products?** Explain the demonstrated adaptation/practice loop; acknowledge established equipment, gesture, stem, and coaching products. Do not claim a first without a broader search.

**Who pays?** Present an unvalidated hypothesis and next experiment, not invented demand. **What happens on failure?** The user retains the instrument; assistance becomes unavailable or is cancelled explicitly.

**What did you build today?** Point to commits, components, assets, tests, and demos. Do not include this pre-implementation planning packet or prior splitter as newly built application capability.
